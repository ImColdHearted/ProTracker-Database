Mega Stones Guide

Mega Stone Bracelet Requirements

  • Kanto Champion
  • OT Pokemon that can mega-evolve

Obtaining The Mega Bracelet

In order to get the Mega Bracelet, you have to talk to Assistant Theodor in Vermilion City Library which is directly east of the Pokemon Center.

After talking to him, you now need to show him a self caught (OT) Pokemon that has the ability to mega-evolve. It can be a previously-caught Pokemon.

The shown Pokemon has to fill the requirements:

  • Level 100
  • Max happiness (255)
  • Max EVs (510 EVs, no specific stats needed)
After showing him the Pokemon, he will reward you with a Mega Bracelet.

Now you are able to get the different Mega Stones in the threads down below!

Gyardosite

Requirements

  • Kanto Champion
  • Magikarp with at least 120 IVs or a Shiny Magikarp (must be OT)
  • 6 Water Pokemon (level 100 recommended)

Starting the quest

To start the quest, head to Cerulean City and talk to Lorelei near the Pokemon Center.

After talking to her, go to Route 19 and talk with Misty.

Route 19

After talking to Misty, you now have to complete 3 different trials with different conditions in her water training camp.

Trial Master: Patience

Show him the Magikarp with at least 120 IVs or a shiny Magikarp

Trial Master: Adaptation

Beat him with 6 Water Pokemon (level 100 recommended)

Trial Master: Wisdom

He will tell you a riddle about royalty, whose names are shared, but when they grow up they become Queens and Kings.

This refers to Nidoran female and male. The Trial Master NPC now has to be found in one of the Routes where you can encounter Nidoran.

He can be located in Route 3, 9, 10 or 22.

After you have found him, go back to Route 19 and talk to Misty and prepare to battle against Juan.

You can fight with the Pokemon you wish against him, it does not necessary need to be water type.
